# DQ-02 – Company-Year Uniqueness

## Status

FAILED

## Violations

210

### Breakdown

| Table         | Violations |
| ------------- | ---------- |
| balancesheet  | 138        |
| cashflow      | 46         |
| profitandloss | 26         |

---

## Investigation Findings

Manual inspection was performed on the flagged records.

Examples identified:

### Profit & Loss

ADANIPORTS records appear twice for identical years:

* Mar 2013
* Mar 2014
* Mar 2015
* Mar 2016
* Mar 2017
* Mar 2018
* Mar 2019
* Mar 2020
* Mar 2021
* Mar 2022
* Mar 2023
* Mar 2024

### Balance Sheet

ASIANPAINT records appear twice for identical years.

### Cash Flow

Duplicate company-year records were identified for:

* ABB
* BAJAJ-AUTO

and additional companies.

---

## Root Cause Analysis

The source datasets appear to contain both:

1. Standalone Financial Statements
2. Consolidated Financial Statements

for the same company and financial year.

Because the extracted datasets do not contain a separate `statement_type` field, multiple records exist for the same:

(company_id, year)

combination.

As a result, the uniqueness assumption used by DQ-02 is violated.

---

## Impact

The following business key cannot be treated as unique:

(company_id, year)

Additional metadata will be required in future ETL stages if standalone and consolidated records need to be distinguished.

---

## Recommendation

* Document duplicate records as a known source-data characteristic.
* Investigate whether statement type information exists in the original source files.
* Review uniqueness constraints before database loading.

---

# DQ-03 – Foreign Key Integrity

## Status

FAILED

## Violations

408

---

## Investigation Findings

Foreign key validation compared:

child.company_id

against

companies.id

The following company identifiers were found in financial datasets but were not present in the master company dataset.

Missing Company IDs:

* ULTRACEMCO
* UNIONBANK
* UNITDSPR
* VBL
* VEDL
* WIPRO
* ZOMATO
* ZYDUSLIFE

---

## Master Dataset Review

companies.xlsx

Summary:

* Total Records: 92
* Unique Company IDs: 92

The master company table is incomplete relative to the financial datasets.

---

## Root Cause Analysis

Several companies present in financial statement datasets are absent from the company master dataset.

This results in foreign key violations during validation.

---

## Impact

Database loading with enforced foreign key constraints would fail for affected records.

Future execution of:

PRAGMA foreign_key_check;

would report violations unless the master dataset is corrected.

---

## Recommendation

* Review source extraction process for companies.xlsx.
* Verify whether missing companies should exist in the master dataset.
* Update the company master dataset before production database loading.
